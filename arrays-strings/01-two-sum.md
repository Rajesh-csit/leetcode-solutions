# Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

I used two loops to check every possible pair of numbers in the array. 
For each pair, I checked whether their sum is equal to the given target.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

## Notes

I learned how to use nested loops to compare different pairs of elements.
I also tested the solution locally before submitting it to LeetCode.
